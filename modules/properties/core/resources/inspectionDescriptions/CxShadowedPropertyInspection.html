Reports a property declaration that never applies.

The SAP Commerce property chain is layered, and a declaration in a higher layer beating one in a lower layer is the chain working as designed — local.properties overriding an extension is the entire point of local.properties, and it is not reported. What is reported are the two cases where the losing declaration is a surprise rather than a design:

  • Two extensions declare the same key. Neither is more important than the other by layer, so the winner is decided by the extension load order — something nobody wrote down and which changes when localextensions.xml changes. The value you are reading may simply not be the one that applies.
  • The extension is not listed in localextensions.xml. The platform never loads its project.properties at all, so the declaration is not overridden — it is absent. The file still reads as though it configures something.

Quick fixes open the declaration that actually applies, or remove the one that does not.
